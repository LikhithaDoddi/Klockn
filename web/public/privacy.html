1. Who we are

Klockn ("Klockn", "we", "us", or "our") is a group scheduling and availability platform that helps people find times that work for everyone. Our service is available at klockn.com.

This Privacy Policy describes how Klockn collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our website, mobile application, and services (collectively, the "Service"). By using our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

For questions about this policy, contact us at hello@klockn.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide directly

  • Account information: When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google.
  • Group and contact information: When you create a group or invite others, we collect the email addresses you provide.
  • Communications: If you contact us for support, we collect the contents of your messages.

Information we collect automatically

  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, time spent, and actions taken within the Service.
  • Device and browser information: Browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and IP address.
  • Log data: Server logs including timestamps, errors, and referring URLs, retained for security and debugging.

Calendar data (when you connect Google Calendar)

See Section 3 for a full explanation of exactly what calendar data we access, how we use it, and how it is stored.

3. Google API data — what we access and why

Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Klockn's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we request access to

When you connect your Google Calendar, Klockn requests the following OAuth scope:

Scope What it allows Why we need it
calendar.readonly Read-only access to your Google Calendar data To determine when you are free or busy, so your group can find a time that works for everyone

What we actually read

✅  We see: Time blocks when you are marked as free or busy (start time, end time, busy/free status)

❌  We never see: Event titles, event descriptions, event locations, attendee names or email addresses, meeting notes, attachments, recurrence patterns, or any other event content

Klockn queries the Google Calendar freebusy API, which by design returns only free/busy intervals — not event details. This is a deliberate technical and product decision to protect your privacy.

How we use Google Calendar data

  • To show your free/busy availability to members of groups you have joined
  • To find shared free windows across group members
  • To power AI-assisted scheduling suggestions within your group

What we do not do with Google data

  • We do not use Google data to serve advertisements
  • We do not sell or transfer Google data to third parties
  • We do not use Google data for any purpose not described in this policy
  • We do not allow humans to read your Google data except when you explicitly request support and grant permission
  • We do not use Google data for credit assessment, lending, or insurance purposes

Refresh tokens and storage

To keep your free/busy data current without requiring you to reconnect each time, Klockn stores an encrypted OAuth refresh token. This token is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and is used solely to refresh your free/busy availability periodically. You can revoke this access at any time from your Google account settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by disconnecting within the Klockn app.

6. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on servers located in the United States (AWS us-east-1). We implement the following security measures:

  • Encryption at rest: Google Calendar refresh tokens are encrypted using AES-256-GCM before storage
  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted to and from our Service uses TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Access controls: Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel and requires multi-factor authentication
  • Monitoring: We monitor for anomalous activity and maintain security logs

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you discover a security vulnerability, please contact us at hello@klockn.com.

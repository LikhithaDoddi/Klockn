How it works

Simple as sending
a message.

No more "when is everyone free?" Klockn already knows. It tells you.

01
Connect your calendar
Link Google or Apple Calendar. We only read free or busy — never event names, who you're meeting, or any details.
02
Build your groups
Family. College friends. Work crew. Weekend people. Name your groups and invite them once — they're connected forever.
03
AI finds the moment
Klockn watches real-time availability across your groups and surfaces the window when everyone's actually free. Then nudges you.
04
Book everything, together
Say yes, start a conversation, and Klockn books the hotel, dinner, tickets, rides — for everyone — all at once.
